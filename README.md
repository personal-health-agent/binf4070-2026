# BINF 4070 / COMS 4995 W008 Labs

This repository contains student lab materials for **The Future of Personal Health Assistant: Multimodal Health Data Management Agents**, offered in Fall 2026 by Columbia DBMI and Columbia CS.

Weekly lab notebooks and their required public data or assets are organized under `week-NN/`. Open the assigned notebook in Google Colab, then select **File → Save a copy in Drive** before you begin so that your work is saved in your own account.

Course information and weekly materials are available on the [course website](https://personal-health-agent.github.io/).

Solutions and instructor-only materials are not distributed in this repository.
